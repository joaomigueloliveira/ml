## BFCL along the run

Explanation only (#70): these scores never choose a checkpoint or a method, and the selected checkpoints (in bold) keep their official numbers. Agreement with the 2B is the share of BFCL rows where the checkpoint makes the same calls as the raw 2B, both parsed; it rises with accuracy alone, since two right answers agree. Copying the 2B's errors counts the rows where the 2B's parsed answer is wrong and the checkpoint makes the same calls: that one separates imitating the teacher from getting better. The intervals overlap almost everywhere; compare two checkpoints with a paired test, not by overlap.
