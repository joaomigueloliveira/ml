## Starting line for Phase 2

On test-clean the student scores **55.1** and the teacher **76.9**: a gap of **21.8 points**. #2's success criterion is to recover at least half of it, a distilled 0.8B at **66.0** or better on test-clean, with the recovered fraction significant at p < 0.05. How that is tested (a paired interval on the recovered fraction, and against which student baseline) is settled in the Phase 2 design review.
