# Serving cost

Issue #63. Generated by `uv run --no-sync fc-serve --report` from the runs in `runs/`; numbers and provenance (model revisions, commit): [`serving.json`](serving.json). vLLM 0.33.0 on one RTX 3060 12 GB with its default scheduler (chunked prefill, 8,192 batched tokens, prefix caching) and fc-eval's settings: bf16, greedy, the eval prompt, thinking off, at most 1,024 new tokens, `gpu_memory_utilization` 0.85. Every test-clean prompt is submitted at once. Cheaper means fewer GPU-seconds per 1,000 requests, each model at its best cap on concurrent requests.

**The Qwen/Qwen3.5-0.8B serves 1.9x cheaper than the Qwen/Qwen3.5-2B**, which misses #2's 2x criterion.

- Per output token it is 1.86x: the two models' answers differ in length, and a request's cost includes its answer.
- One request at a time, over the same 50 prompts, it is 2.4x faster. Batching narrows the gap: the workload is mostly prompt processing (input tokens per request below).
- Measured with vLLM's default scheduler; a larger batched-token budget was not tried, and could favour the smaller model.
