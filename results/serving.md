# Serving cost

Issue #63. Generated by `uv run --no-sync fc-serve --report` from the runs in `runs/`; numbers: [`serving.json`](serving.json). vLLM on one RTX 3060 12 GB, fc-eval's settings (bf16, greedy, the eval prompt, thinking off, at most 1,024 new tokens, `gpu_memory_utilization` 0.85), on all test-clean prompts. Cheaper means fewer GPU-seconds per 1,000 requests, each model at its best cap on concurrent requests.

**The Qwen/Qwen3.5-0.8B serves 1.9x cheaper than the Qwen/Qwen3.5-2B**, which misses #2's 2x criterion.
